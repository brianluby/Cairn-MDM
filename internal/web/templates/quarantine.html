Quarantine

Enrollments held after an ambiguous certificate outcome: the CA may have signed, so Cairn refuses to retry rather than risk a second live certificate. Each hold is released only through four evidence-gated steps (fence, enumerate, confirm rejected, release). Nothing is reissued; the device enrolls again with a new link.
